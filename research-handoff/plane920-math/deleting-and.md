# Necessary positive lines for the sole surplus AND

Suppose a 26-gate candidate has five OR gates, thirteen AND terminals, and eight hidden ANDs. In the connected case there are thirteen hidden gates on thirteen points, hence one surplus merger E. If E is AND, replacing E by false leaves only seven hidden ANDs and a subgraph of a hidden forest after simplifying gates. Disconnected components can be connected by unread binary OR gates without increasing the AND count. Any target for which its original positive row survives is still correct on the whole promise, because decreasing a wire cannot introduce false positives.

The same assertion holds after replacing E by the OR of any earlier wires in its component which imply E on all allowed rows. Such wires weakly decrease E, and computing their OR creates only surplus OR gates in this component. Its set of previous component vertices does not change, so all later ordinary mergers still join separate components. The hidden cut/obligation lower bound permits arbitrary surplus ORs. In particular take the OR of every raw point whose singleton makes E true, or false if there are none. A target lost under this replacement must be disjoint from all these points.

To test the remaining target family we reran the single-wire-obligation recurrence, retaining only its actual targets. Numerical lower bounds for the original full family are used on a region only when all omitted slices have size at most two (and impose no constraints in the free-helper relaxation). Six- through nine-point subfamily minima can be imposed from actual local RUP receipts. Early witnesses of the recurrence can be refined by inspecting the exact two-wire demands *and* the single-wire obligations in their small components. At a region U with local budget k, small-core.js models k hidden ANDs with arbitrary free ORs. For an obligatory slice its own active output can select only one raw or hidden AND, whereas an ordinary target can select two. The original target-specific downset is checked independently for every slice. An UNSAT answer blocks the corresponding local state and all supersets of its obligatory signals with no larger budget. SAT and UNKNOWN are never used to rule out states.

For a fixed omitted family, refine-deletion.js transfers each checked rejection across the verified stabilizer of the omitted lines. transfer-certs.js also imports certificates from different omitted families: a verified permutation may be used only if every nontrivial ordinary target and every nontrivial assigned mandatory target from the originating local CNF is still present. New mandatory-line labels are permuted with the points. The ordinary target list of an originating CNF is precisely its retained slices of size at least three; mandatory slices of size two are separately included. Each applied bound is therefore a relaxation for the new state. The transfer tables are finite derived data; replay always starts from concrete input CNFs and positive-hint proof streams.

For seven hidden ANDs the forest recurrence rejects every family obtained by dropping at most three of the thirteen lines. One omitted line has one orbit; two omitted lines have one orbit; triples are either concurrent (representative omission mask 35) or a triangle (mask 7). For three omissions the files audit-obligations-1-7-drop35.json and audit-obligations-1-7-drop7.json record the final negative decisions. The corresponding refinement catalogs contain 65 and 240 local UNSAT receipts respectively. Unresolved local attempts were not used. For mask 7, transferred-7.json records the additional geometrically valid imported bounds. The no-case recurrence rejects the representative even while ignoring the unresolved local attempts. Geometric orbit reduction is checked by plane-orbits.js.

Consequently E must remain indispensable on at least four positive lines, even under the sufficient-signal replacement above. The union of four distinct projective lines has at least ten points: start with four times four incidences and subtract at most one for each of their six pairwise meetings. Thus E can be true on at most three raw singleton rows. Deleting an entire four-line pencil (mask 5137) also fails at seven forest ANDs; this further excludes a pencil as the *only* set of four losses. The other four-line geometries need separate testing.
