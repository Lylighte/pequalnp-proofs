# A seven-point component needs three hidden AND gates

For any seven-point set U, the target for each line L after freezing outside U has a unique positive input S=L intersect U among U-inputs of size at most |S|. Consider the relaxed model with freely available OR gates, two hidden AND gates H1,H2 in order, and a further distinct binary AND for each target. A real U component in the surplus-OR reduction embeds in this model: constants and copy gates can be removed; a terminal reducing to a single signal can duplicate the signal. Every input to an AND is an OR of raw points and earlier hidden ANDs. At a target's single positive row an active signal can be chosen on each side of its terminal: keeping just the two chosen signals only decreases the output and preserves its positive row. Thus for any target of size at least three, the two chosen signals are raw points or H1,H2 and cannot both be raw.

Write n_i for the number of full plane lines meeting U in i points. Counting point-line incidences and distinct pairs gives sum(i n_i)=28 and sum(binomial(i,2) n_i)=21. If n_4=0 these imply n_1=3n_3−14, so there are at least five three-point targets. If n_4=1 and n_3=0, the pair identity gives n_2=15 although just twelve other lines exist. Thus either there is a four-point target and another target of size at least three, or there are at least five three-point targets.

**The four-point case.** Fix consistent true OR operands on its four-point positive row. Its AND-only proof needs all four source points and hence at least three ANDs. The two hidden ANDs and its own terminal must all occur. After suppressing OR paths there are four sources and three AND gates with six operand edges; the connected proof is a tree. In particular every true branching gate in this proof has a unique minimum witness consisting of its two or more descendant points: substitute any globally minimum witness and use the untouched outside leaves. Their union must still be the four-point target, so the witness is exactly the subtree support. Thus the minimum witnesses of both hidden gates belong to this four-point line. On any different slice S of size at least three, the earliest hidden AND that is true would have an accepting input of size at most two contained in S, because all earlier hidden ANDs are false there. The mandatory minimum would then have two points in the intersection of two different lines, impossible. The terminal without a true hidden AND cannot isolate three or more points.

**The triple case.** A target computed by a hidden signal alone can instead be paired with any one of its own raw points. A conjunction of a raw p with a signal g of minimum degree at most two can isolate at most one of the linear family of triples. Indeed any minimum true witness B for g gives B union {p} of size at most three and this sufficient input must equal the triple. Thus g has a unique minimum pair, common to every such target, and two distinct line slices cannot share it. H1 has minimum degree at most two whenever satisfiable. If H2 does too, the two groups with a raw point and the one fixed conjunction H1 AND H2 can isolate at most three distinct triples.

The remaining possibility is that H2 is used and has minimum true degree three. At least one of its two OR operands cannot mention a raw point, since choosing a raw point on each side would give a witness of size at most two. That side is H1; the opposite side cannot contain H1, since that would make H2 identical to H1. Accordingly H2 is the conjunction of three nonempty raw ORs with point sets C,D,E (two from H1, the third from the other operand). They must be pairwise disjoint, again because H2 has no two-point witness. Its minimum triples are the entire Cartesian product with one representative from each of C,D,E. A conjunction H2 AND p can isolate a triple only when the two classes not containing p are both singletons. If exactly two classes are singletons, all isolated triples using H2 share their two fixed points and hence at most one belongs to the line family. If all three classes are singletons there is just one available triple anyway. H1 AND H2 equals H2 and creates no additional option. This cannot give five triples. An unsatisfiable helper can simply be removed.

## Independent finite checks and root budget

small-core.js encodes the two hidden AND inputs as unrestricted ORs over raw and earlier helper signals. It requires a correct conjunction pair separately for each target of size at least three, using every input row up to that target's size. The 48 orbits under the 39 maps x ↦ 3^i x + t cover all 1,716 seven-point sets. All returned UNSAT, and replay-small-core.js regenerated positive-hint LRAT and checked every proof from its CNF with the independent streaming RUP checker. small-core/replay.json records 54,361 total additions and all 48 final empty clauses. The elementary proof above does not need these certificates.

The same encoder gives the stronger computational bound of **four** helpers on every seven-point component: all 48 three-helper instances are UNSAT with 408,706 independently replayed positive-hint additions in small-core/replay-3.json. With four helpers the representative U=499 is SAT; it has exactly two four-point slices and all other slices have size at most two. Two auxiliary ANDs per four-point output give a direct construction. For six-point components all 1,716 need at least two helpers, and 1,638 of them need at least three; the other 78 are SAT at two. The independent six-point receipts have 8,806 and 34,078 additions respectively. Per-orbit masks and verdicts are in small-core/results-6-1.json and results-6-2.json.

Using all 5,616 verified incidence-preserving frame maps, there are only four types of seven-point sets. Four hidden helpers are still impossible in the 234, 936 and 468 orbits represented by masks 127, 949 and 381. Their LRAT replays add 177,676, 76,625 and 218,800 clauses, respectively (small-core/replay-seven-four.json). Thus 1,638 of the seven-point sets require at least five helpers; the remaining 78 have the preceding exact four-helper construction. For six points the orbit of 234 sets represented by 427 also fails at three helpers; the other three orbits are SAT at three. The independently replayed three-helper six-point record is small-core/deleted/6-3-0.json.

To propagate the seven-point lower bound, track a subset of target lines as obligations at each component U. Such a line must have one native signal with unique minimum L intersect U, in addition to the usual two-wire demand. For a child region retain each previously mandatory slice of size at least two. If two mandatory lines cross a cut, the split is impossible because a one-wire crossing demand must use its single mixed merger H, whose minimum cannot equal two different crossing slices. If exactly one crosses, H must be AND and the minimum labels of its two drivers are fixed to this line's slices. When no mandatory line crosses, an OR merger forces both native child obligations on every cross-line, while an AND merger allows the two fixed-label exceptions described in the cut proof. For matching both exceptions, retain the native obligation on at least one side. At each split divide the available AND budget between its two children, paying one for an AND merger. All unwaived unique labels charge their subset size minus one in their own component, independently across different lines.

This induction applies after point-specific outside freezing. In particular a downstream surplus OR can be fixed to its true branch for a single target, giving a monotone lower output with the same positive row. A previously required native signal with unique minimum on its slice keeps its slice after this substitution, and therefore projects to a single native child signal whenever it must still depend on the child. This remains valid for a unique minimum output specified on its cardinality downset.

budget-obligations.js --plain and audit-obligations.js seven 8 independently reject the full thirteen-point region with zero initial hard obligations and eight ANDs. The latter uses the pre-verified plain cardinality floors at other region sizes, checks the elementary seven-point bound, and tests 37,386 memoized states. It accepts the relaxation with nine ANDs and accepts the eight-AND relaxation when the new seven-point bound is disabled. These positive controls are not constructions of circuits. The known 26-gate candidate with a connected single-cycle hidden graph and a cycle-sink OR would have at most eight hidden ANDs once four ORs have been excluded, so this entire branch is eliminated. The case in which every cycle sink is AND is separate.
