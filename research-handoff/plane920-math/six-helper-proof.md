# Six hidden AND gates cannot realize the thirteen plane targets, even with free OR

In the dual problem an input is a subset of the 13 points. On the promised downset of subsets of size at most four, target i accepts exactly L_i = i + {0,1,3,9} modulo 13. The argument shows at least seven nonterminal AND gates in addition to the thirteen output AND gates. The proof below uses finite certificates independently checked by RUP and two small combinatorial enumerations. It is a local mathematical result and has no Lean formalization in this directory.

## Model and elementary facts

Constants, unary gates and repeated copies can be folded. Each target has a distinct terminal AND. Indeed an OR computing the target on the promise can be followed backwards along a child true at its positive input. That child stays false on all the negative inputs. Continuing this way cannot end at a raw point. Any refs to the chosen terminal gates can then be removed from the hidden computation by substituting false. On L_i every other terminal was already false, while the terminal for i is downstream of all its own inputs. This preserves the thirteen positive values and only decreases other values. Restore each terminal at the outputs as a distinct sink. Every remaining AND has two sides that are ORs over raw inputs and preceding hidden AND gates. At a target terminal choose one signal on each side that is true on L_i. Their conjunction alone realizes this target on the downset. A conjunction of the same signal twice can be replaced by that signal paired with any p in L_i. A pair of raw points cannot isolate a four-set.

For a hidden gate g write R(g) for the set of target indices where it is true. It is low if it accepts some input of size at most three and high otherwise. A low gate g that supplies a raw pair p AND g for L must accept exactly one input of size at most three, namely T = L minus p. For any such accepted U, the input U union {p} must equal L, forcing U=T. Thus a low gate covers at most one target by a raw pair because distinct plane lines share at most one point. The first hidden AND cannot supply a raw pair, since a satisfiable conjunction of two ORs of raw points accepts some input of size at most two.

Each high g used with p on L needs p private for L in R(g). In symbols the only member of R(g) incident with p is L. Let P(R) be the number of members with at least one private point in the line set R. Exhaustion of the 8192 line masks gives the following maxima. They can equally be reproduced by iterating over each of 13 raw points, contributing its unique member of R when its pencil intersects R in just one member, and counting the distinct contributed members.

## Auxiliary lower bounds and their finite checks

Any one complete line requires two hidden ANDs besides its own terminal. In a consistent accepting proof pick one true child at each OR. The connected proof requires its four distinct raw sources, so needs three binary ANDs including the terminal. Any pair of complete lines needs at least four hidden ANDs and any triple needs at least five. Restrict the raw point set to the union of the lines under consideration by fixing outside inputs to false. All pairs are isomorphic. Triples are triangles or trios through a common point. Masks 3, 7, 35 represent the pair and the two triple types respectively. The certificates under small-core/deleted are family-3-3-s-20000, family-7-4-s-125000, and family-35-4-s-125000. Their RUP checkers reached an empty clause after 7024, 74046, and 112426 additions respectively.

A hidden gate false on all selected positive lines can be set permanently to false without changing any of those positives. It can only shrink the terminal output on the negative inputs. Consequently, if there are six available helpers, every pair of helpers is jointly false on at most two target lines, since three such lines would have only four helpers. Every triple of helpers is jointly false on at most one line, since two such lines would have only three helpers. Every single line has at least two active helpers. In particular any two helpers jointly cover at least eleven of the thirteen lines.

At least four helpers are low. Temporarily replace every high by false. On any input of size at most three this preserves all gate values. For each L take the earliest high that was true on L, or take the terminal for L if no high was true. In either case choose one true raw or low signal on each side. The chosen signals remain true on L after deleting the highs, because every earlier high was false. Nowhere on a size-at-most-three input could those two signals simultaneously be true. Hence the circuit consisting of the low helpers alone must offer for each L an endpoint pair true there and false on all inputs of size at most three. Other four-sets and other target lines are allowed to be true for this pair. All lows remain low at their original small witnesses. With three low helpers this relaxed cover is UNSAT, and with four it is SAT. See small-core/deleted/family-8191-3-s-cover4-140000 and family-8191-4-s-cover4-300000. The three-helper certificate has 67606 checked RUP additions. Circuits with fewer than three low gates can be padded by a raw copy. One can likewise pad any counterexample with fewer than six helpers before applying this proof.

The encoder small-core.js uses unrestricted operand ORs. It sorts each binary gate's two symmetric ports. Its local necessity constraints use the following cut argument on a positive input L. For each child signal true at L, record the points q of a given subset S of L whose individual deletion makes that signal false. At a selected true OR child the recorded set contains the parent's recorded set. At an AND the two children's sets have union equal to the parent's set. If the output conjunction is sensitive to all of S, trace these labels backward along a consistent choice of OR sides. Treat each gate with zero or one relevant label as a leaf. Connecting the remaining subgraph to the root requires at least |S|-1 branching gates with two or more labels, since it reaches at least |S| separately labelled leaves. At most one such branch is the final conjunction. Thus among the actual helpers there are at least |S|-2 true gates with at least two deletion-sensitive labels in S. This proves the extra clauses for these complete-line/cover certificates. The allowed final pair in the cover certificate is free and may be different on each line.

## At most four targets can use two helper endpoints

Choose one valid endpoint pair for each of the thirteen terminals, handling repeated signals as raw pairs as above. Suppose k of the pairs are made of two distinct helpers. They are distinct unordered edges of a graph on the six helpers. At the positive row for an edge e the active helper set induces exactly e in this graph. At each other row it induces the empty graph. This follows from each selected endpoint conjunction having exactly one positive target row.

The masks in pair-edges.js enumerate all six-vertex simple graphs and all 64 possible active sets. For each edge and for the unmarked rows it suffices to check inclusion-maximal active masks inducing exactly that edge or no edge respectively. Enlarging a row's active mask cannot raise its consumption of any joint-absence bound. At each row require at least two active helpers, and use the capacities of two on each of fifteen pairs and one on each of twenty triples. The scalar early rejection compares the sum of per-row minimum pair costs, each equal to the number of pairs among the inactive vertices, with the total capacity of 30. The residual backtrack tries every permitted row mask and decrements each relevant capacity. Relabeling the six vertices preserves these tests. All graphs with k from 5 to 13 fail. The k=4 control passes for 30 labeled graphs, all four-leaf stars (orbit representative 75 with the program's edge numbering). No structural property of those stars is needed. In fact it suffices to verify all five-edge graphs because selecting any five of a larger collection would still give five single-edge target rows and eight edgeless rows. Thus at least nine terminal pairs are raw pairs.

## The high helpers and their positions

Six lows cannot supply nine raw pairs. With five lows and one high H, at most four raw pairs come from the lows, so H supplies at least five. The private-point table gives |R(H)| at most six. On its at least seven negative target rows, any two other helpers are simultaneously active on at least three, because each can miss at most two in common with H. They therefore cannot be an endpoint pair for any target. Consequently those seven or more rows would all need a low plus a raw point. Four such targets are the maximum. This excludes the one-high case. The four-low bound leaves exactly four lows and two highs A,B.

At most three of the four lows can supply raw pairs. At least six raw pairs come from the highs, so designate a high A responsible for at least three. Its positive set has at most seven rows by the private-point table. On the at least six rows outside R(A), any pair of other helpers is true together at least twice, and thus cannot serve as an endpoint pair. All those rows require a raw pair. At most three can be assigned to the lows, so B also supplies at least three raw pairs and has at most seven positive rows. The symmetric argument shows every two-helper endpoint pair must include B as well as A. There is at most one such target. We now know there are at least twelve raw targets.

Each high H is paired with a raw point on at least three different targets. Those three targets alone can be computed by keeping H and its complete predecessor circuit and using three separate terminals p_i AND H. The three-line certificate requires five hidden ANDs in this circuit, including H. Hence each high occurs in one of slots five and six. Both slots must be used by the two highs. In particular every low signal, including the last low, is computable using at most four ANDs with arbitrary free OR operands. This argument does not interchange a low and a high gate.

## A low raw partner of rank at most four is supported on at most four target rows

Let g be such a partner and T its unique true triple, contained in its assigned target L. Every other line M on which g is true meets L in exactly one point. If M misses T, restricting outside T union M to false gives a circuit with four or fewer ANDs accepting the disjoint triple and four-set, while rejecting all other triples on their seven coordinates. This is impossible. The unrestricted four-AND encoder triple-quartet.js obtains 19231 checked RUP additions to the empty clause for intersection size zero. The intersection-size-one variant is SAT at four ANDs.

If instead two other true lines meet T at different points, their mutual intersection is outside L. Up to permuting the raw coordinates, the three positives are {0,1,2}, {0,3,4,5}, {1,3,6,7}. Fix all other coordinates to false. The gate must reject the other 55 triples on these eight points. The same unrestricted gate model in triple-two-quartets.js yields an empty clause after 12492 checked RUP additions with four ANDs, and SAT with five. The model allows arbitrary earlier gates and arbitrary ORs in each AND operand. It requires just the three indicated positives and non-T triples as negatives. If fewer than four ANDs occur, identity ANDs can pad a single target signal up to the fourth slot.

It follows that every true line of g other than L meets T at the same point. Such lines together with L are contained in a single four-line pencil. When there are no other true lines the stated bound also holds.

## Contradiction

Of the at least twelve raw targets, at most three are low raw targets. Consequently one of the two highs supplies at least five raw targets, and its positive set has at most six rows. Every other helper must have at least five positive rows because its union with this high contains at least eleven. The previous paragraph makes any low raw partner impossible. All twelve or more raw targets come from just the two highs. Each can supply at most six, and six private lines occur only when its positive set has size six. Thus there must be exactly twelve raw targets, six assigned to each high. The two positive sets are precisely those two disjoint sets of six assigned target rows. Their conjunction cannot supply the thirteenth target. This excludes six helpers.

## Reproduction and independent checks

Use the bundled Node executable from this directory, e.g. ../../tools/node/node.exe. Rebuild the subfamily and cover proofs using free-family.js with arguments matching the file stems and --prune, adding --cover4 for the low-cover instance. Each UNSAT result is checked by ../plane920-search/stream-rup.cjs. Run pair-edges.js for the graph table. The private-point maxima are also in small-core/high-pairs-direct.json, independently calculated by pencil incidence. Run triple-quartet.js and triple-two-quartets.js for the final small certificates and their SAT controls. The unneeded two-high joint enumerations high-pairs.js and high-pairs-direct.js agree on 8658 labeled pairs in six oriented orbit representatives after prioritizing the high with more available private rows. These joint enumerations are ancillary controls. The proof just given uses only the pointwise private maxima.
