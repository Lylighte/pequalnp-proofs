# Seven prescribed line outputs cannot use five ORs and eight helper ANDs

If fewer than five ORs occur, six of the seven outputs already contradict four-or-six-nine.md. Write T for the seven chosen line inputs. The following requirements hold for every OR of a candidate circuit.

1. Each child must be true on at least two rows of T where the other child is false. Otherwise bypass the OR toward its other child and retain six complete line outputs with four ORs and eight helpers.
2. A genuinely paid child must be true on at least three rows of T. If it is true on at most two, bypass the OR and set the generating AND to zero, keeping five complete lines with four ORs and seven helpers. This contradicts five-lines-seven-helpers.md. Thus each arm can be written as one optional raw point conjoined with earlier named ORs. Indeed any nonraw, nonnamed arm is a flattened AND true on three distinct plane lines, and cannot contain two distinct fixed raw points.
3. Distinct ORs cannot have the same two-element exclusive set in their pairs of children on T. Choosing for each OR the opposite child retains the five targets outside that pair. In topological order, each bypass preserves the original value at every gate on those five positives, and monotonicity preserves all negative requirements. The remaining three ORs and eight helpers contradict three-or-six-lines-29.md.
4. At most three hidden ANDs can be private on the seven targets in total. Deleting four such gates and their at most four outputs would compute three targets with four helpers, contrary to the free-OR three-line bound. The same bound applies to the sum of lower costs of gates private with respect to all thirteen geometric positives.

The four seven-line family orbits under the 5,616 explicit projective collineations have masks 127, 191, 247 and 415. For each type, normalize the ordered three-OR function prefixes by successive stabilizers, using requirements 1 and 2. This gives respectively 533, 1,350, 1,727 and 175 prefixes. Enumerate the fourth and fifth ORs using full-domain functions. Each allowed child is an optional raw factor times a subset of earlier named ORs; a genuinely paid function active on only two targets is omitted. The counted fourth-OR functions in the four types are 20,742, 43,587, 68,394 and 5,365.

For a fixed prefix and a line L the strongest conjunction of all named ORs true at L with two, three or four raw points from L gives a lower bound of zero, one or two private ANDs, respectively. This follows by cutting at named ORs, raw points and shared ANDs, the latter containing at most one raw point in their own named-OR factorization. An available terminal plus t private ANDs has at most t+2 boundary factors. All four raw points always suffice. The seven-line sum must be at most three.

The cheap fourth-OR screen uses the fact that an active fifth arm has at most one raw factor. In order to make a fourth-prefix line zero-private, that line must already be isolable with at most three raw points. At least four lines must be zero-private at the end. For a proposed last arm with raw point p, improving a line to zero-private requires that it was already zero-private, or that some isolating triple for the earlier prefix contains p. Improving it to one-private likewise requires it already cost at most one, or p in the line. Each active child of the final OR must pass its own condition. After this screen the actual final OR is evaluated on all 1,093 allowed inputs for every selected line, followed by the independently implemented per-line shared-factor calculation.

Only 60 function prefixes survive, all for family mask 247. The screens up to this point may combine alternative decompositions with the same complete truth vector because they only use the ordered OR values. For these 60 prefixes, enumerate every decomposition of each of the five physical ORs as two products of earlier ORs and optional raw points. Check requirements 1, 2 and 3 on the fixed family simultaneously. No full choice satisfies the three necessary conditions. No SAT result or final paid-library result is needed for these 60.
