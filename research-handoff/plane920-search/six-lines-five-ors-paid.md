# A necessary two-line paid arm with six targets, five ORs and seven helpers

This is a conditional reduction. Suppose a circuit computes six complete targets with five ORs and seven hidden AND gates. Both children of every OR must have at least two exclusively true target rows. Otherwise bypassing that OR leaves five full targets with four ORs and seven helpers, excluded by five-lines-seven-helpers.md. Likewise, at most two physical helpers can be private to their own targets in total. Removing three such helpers and the at most three affected terminals would leave at least three targets with only four helpers.

Suppose first that every genuinely paid OR arm is true on at least three of the six targets. For each of the four six-line incidence types use the projection and exact per-line isolation checks in seven-lines-five-ors.md. The resulting counts of normalized three-OR prefixes are 50, 329, 37 and 136. After 10,544 fourth-OR extensions, the complete-domain tests leave sixty five-OR prefixes on type 243 and none on the other three types. For these sixty, enumerate **all** actual five-OR decompositions. On six targets repeated exclusive pairs are allowed. Each function prefix admitted one such decomposition. The physical paid-library and subfamily-deletion bounds exclude all sixty at seven helpers. Reproduce locally with six-target-last-or.cjs 100000 --pay3 --all and refine-seven-five.cjs --six. The outputs are six-target-last-or-100000-pay3.json and refine-six-five-pay3.json.

Therefore a hypothetical circuit contains a paid OR arm H true on exactly two targets E. At least two are necessary from the exclusive-set lower bound. Its sister is false on both. Bypass this OR and delete H. The four surviving targets now have four ORs and six helpers. This budget is tight at both gate types: four targets with three ORs and six helpers are excluded by five-lines-seven-helpers.md. Four targets with four ORs and five helpers are also impossible. Indeed any helper true on fewer than two of those four targets could be deleted with one line, producing three targets/four helpers, and otherwise every helper is shared and every terminal needs zero private factors. The four-OR zero-private bound in five-lines-27.md allows at most three distinct targets. If a subfamily OR were positively dominated, the three-OR bound applies instead.

It follows that neither original target in E has a private AND. Such a gate would also be removable on the four survivors and is distinct from H. No different physical helper can be true only inside E, and no different OR can have E as one of its exclusive sets on the six targets. These would respectively permit one more helper deletion or another OR bypass. The full six-target/seven-helper impossibility is still open in the two-line-arm branch.
