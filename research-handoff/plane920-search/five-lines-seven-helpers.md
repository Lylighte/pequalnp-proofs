# Five complete line targets require more than seven helper ANDs with at most four ORs

The domain and terminal convention are the same as in six-lines-28.md. This strengthens the previous four-OR, five-target, six-helper bound by one. Consequently a 28-gate circuit with eight ORs cannot have any resolution of its first four OR gates that preserves five line positives. Its first four ORs fall into the earlier catalogue of 1,217 exceptional prefixes.

## Three targets with two ORs and five helper ANDs

The unrestricted one-OR theorem requires six helpers on three targets. The free-OR three-target theorem requires five. Thus any candidate here uses exactly both ORs and all five helpers, even after bypassing duplicates and gates immaterial to the target positives. In particular an OR arm must be exclusively true on at least one selected line. Every helper must be true on at least one target. No line has two private helpers, since removing these and this line would leave two complete targets with three helpers, contrary to the free-OR pair certificate. A pure monomial of three or more raw points cannot be the first OR's arm. Its generating AND cone would contain two distinct nontrivial pure monomials on the same line, both private, as different lines meet in at most one point.

There are two geometric types of selected triples, represented by masks 7 and 35. In the first OR enumerate both operands as nonempty raw subsets of size one or two in the selected lines. Remove selections with comparable positive sets and use the stabilizer of the selected triple. For the second OR each arm is an arbitrary raw subset times optionally the first OR, since all intervening gates are ANDs. The raw subset must be contained in a selected line on which the arm is true. The pure first-OR option is also allowed. This enumeration includes arms with two or more compulsory raw points. Programs with only singleton monomials throughout are already excluded by few-or-2-3-5.json.

Apply the per-line three-raw-point necessary filter. Next charge one physical gate for each distinct input to either OR whose function is neither a raw point nor an earlier OR. Grant each charged function as a free output-cone boundary after charging its physical cost. For a charged helper with multiple positive lines also grant the strongest possible shared factor for its exact positive mask. Add possible further shared libraries, compute remaining private costs, and apply the one-/two-/three-line deletion test of six-lines-28.md. The resulting 79 and 2 candidates respectively are tested by forward search over the five physical AND slots. Generate ANDs from already available signals; enable each fixed OR for free as soon as its two particular operands are available. Keep functions true on a selected line, since a useless gate would contradict the bounds just cited. On each search state, check whether each target appears as an intersection of a pair of available wires, including repetitions. All intersections, target comparisons and deduplication take place on the full domain.

Only two normalized first/second OR descriptions, both for nonconcurrent targets (mask 7), admit a circuit. Across all solutions they each have three possible sets of five helper truth functions. In each of these six sets every AND function has exactly one proper decomposition into two available functions from the final set or the raw points. The OR decompositions are fixed by enumeration. Thus these six semantic sets also describe all surviving physical wiring up to interchange of independent gates. Data are in two-or-three-lines-all.json, two-or-three-lines-physical.json and three-line-five-cores.json.

## Four targets with three ORs and six helper ANDs

If all physical OR arms have at most one compulsory raw point, apply the three-OR catalogue in few-or-3-4-6.json. Of 4,483 normalized programs just three pass the original library test. Each exceptional OR program has just one decomposition into paid operand sets. Charging its two genuinely paid operands by paid-or-arms.cjs excludes all three.

Otherwise take the earliest OR X with an operand forcing two raw points. Its operand H is generated by a helper AND, and accepts at most one prescribed line L. Bypass X to its other operand B and set H to zero. If H is false on all four, the remaining four outputs with two ORs and five helpers in particular solve four outputs at two ORs and six helpers, already excluded in few-or-2-4-6.json and five-lines-27.md. Otherwise leave out L. The three remaining targets require the full five ANDs and both remaining ORs, as above. H therefore cannot feed any other surviving internal gate directly. Another AND fed by H would become false; another OR fed by H would become unary. Either would contradict the exact lower bound for the three remaining outputs. The surviving gates likewise cannot become duplicate wires.

Reconstruct the four-target program from each of the six physical three-target cores. The input of H can be any two distinct raw or core wires. B can be any of these wires. In any subset of the core ports that use B, replace B by X. Define H as the chosen conjunction and X as H OR B, and reject cycles in the reconstructed graph. Repetitions in H would give a redundant gate in the original reduced circuit. The new targets' terminal ports are unrestricted; test every pair of available wires for exact equality to each singleton line row. Require H false on the old three positives and true on the new fourth line. These 39,520 templates give 34,864 acyclic graphs, of which 13,952 pass the nontrivial H test. None can supply the four terminals. This reconstruction also excludes any four-target candidate having an OR arm produced by an AND true on at most one of the four targets, irrespective of whether it forces two raw points.

## Five targets with four ORs and seven helper ANDs

If a genuine paid OR arm is true on at most one prescribed line, bypass the OR and delete its arm and that output, obtaining the excluded four-target, three-OR, six-helper case. Choosing the first arm with two compulsory raw points if necessary likewise eliminates the unrestricted monomial case. Hence the shared-arm three-OR enumeration in six-lines-28.md applies.

If an output line has two private helpers, no other selected line has a private helper. Otherwise deleting those three gates and the two outputs leaves three complete targets with only four helpers, which is impossible. The remaining four outputs would therefore have no private helper. Four ORs can support at most three zero-private lines: four-or-free-intersection.cjs checked this using the strongest four-OR factor at each line for all normalized positive-incomparable fourth ORs. If the fourth OR is positively dominated, bypass it to obtain the previously excluded five-target, three-OR, seven-helper case. Thus each selected line has at most one private helper.

Enumerate all 1,897 three-OR prefix classes and 1,675,184 pairs of fourth-OR inputs. Of the 1,477,787 positive-incomparable pairs, 406,240 pass the preliminary per-line screen. Deduplication gives 397,530 fourth-OR truth functions within their fixed prefixes, of which 386,201 need the full library and subfamily-deletion check. The remaining budget excludes every one. See four-or-5lines-7-shared-0-1897.json.

Run the bundled Node in this directory with each of the following arguments:

```

three-or-five-lines.cjs 3 4 6
paid-or-arms.cjs
two-or-three-lines-all.cjs
check-small-and-program.cjs --cores
lift-small-core.cjs
four-or-six-lines.cjs 0 1897 --five --shared-arms
```

The small incidence base cases and the dependencies of the preceding five-/six-line results are given in six-lines-28.md and five-lines-27.md. The new reductions and finite checks have no Lean formalization.
