Fix a line L. Any signal leading to its terminal, stopping at the raw inputs or a named OR, is a conjunction of such atoms. Cut its AND cone additionally at every AND active on another positive row M. That shared subexpression is a conjunction of atoms all active on L and M. Replace it freely by the conjunction of *every* such atom. This shrinks it but preserves its value at L. The available stronger free expression for each M is their common point together with all ORs active on both lines. These free expressions also cover all raw variables from L, and ORs true on L and another line. Include ORs true only on L separately. Every gate left in the cone is private to L in the sense that it is an AND true on precisely one named positive row, and different lines count disjoint sets of such gates. With t nonterminal private gates, the terminal combines at most t+2 distinct free factors. This holds even for a DAG by counting binary edges and connected vertices in its ancestor cone. If no two free factors isolate L one private helper is needed. If no three do, two helpers are needed. Summing over L and adding the lower bound on shared helpers gives a necessary condition of at most eight.
