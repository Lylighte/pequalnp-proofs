# Necessary OR counts for a 29-gate candidate

At size 29 there are thirteen terminal ANDs and at most 16−h hidden ANDs if there are h dual ORs. The already established lower bound of 29 is in `twenty-nine.md`. The following tests concern the next gate budget.

With zero or one OR the strict one-OR bound requires 26 hidden ANDs. For two ORs see the regular thirteen-line private minimum of 23 in `../plane920-search/two-or-private.json`. A two-point arm can instead be bypassed at a cost of one line, leaving twelve targets and one OR, which needs 24 hidden ANDs. The full 3,370 normalized ordered three-OR operand-pair representatives in `three-prefix-all.cjs` rule out h=3,4,5 by `effective-cut.cjs`; all three counts have zero cutoff hits. Their sharded receipts are `unrestricted/all-three-29-3456-*.jsonl`. Ten or more ORs would leave at most six hidden ANDs, fewer than the unconditional minimum of seven.

## Six OR gates

The following earlier-OR simplification accounts for physical AND gates. In a fixed initial segment choose one input at each successive OR. Keep the set S of target rows where no selected arm lost the value of its original OR. Every wire has its original value on S by topological induction. A compulsory nonraw operand that is distinct from all previous OR values identifies a physical AND before the end of the segment. Distinct operand functions identify distinct AND gates. After the replacements, each such gate is a single raw monomial. If it is true on two rows of S then its support lies in the intersection of two distinct plane lines, so it folds to a raw point or a constant. If it is false throughout a smaller chosen family T contained in S, it can be discarded on T. Both savings can be applied simultaneously. The ORs remaining for T number at most h minus the prefix length; apply the certified partial-family AND lower bound with this OR count. At fixed cardinality of T discard rows with the largest multiplicities of singleton-active paid ANDs to maximize the count of removable helpers.

There is a second, free-OR test. Replace any subset of compulsory AND functions by the disjunctions of the raw singleton minterms they contain. These disjunctions are available from raw inputs alone and are pointwise below their original gates. All original signal values persist on rows outside the union of lost positives. Charge the removed ANDs plus the free-OR helper lower bound on the surviving family. The five- and nine-line bounds are proved in `../plane920-math/partial-helper-proof.md`. Implementation is in `paid-deficits.cjs` and `early-fold.cjs`.

Of the 3,370 starting prefixes, 226 pass these tests. Extend them by the fourth OR with `early-extensions.cjs`. Of 260,209 stabilizer representatives, 9,975 pass the earlier-OR tests and 240 survive the late-OR test. Extend these with `early-fifth.cjs`. Their fifth OR gives 988,170 pairs or 620,370 stabilizer representatives. Of these, 89,217 pass the early test; none survives the final-OR search. All cutoffs are zero. `audit-early-29.cjs` checks the exact path-key join of both levels, recomputes the totals, and compares the optimized row removal against direct enumeration of all retained subsets in 39 comparisons. See `unrestricted/early-29-h6-audit.json`.

Reproduce from this directory with the project Node. For the fourth OR run `early-extensions.cjs 6,7 FROM END 1500` on [0,15), [15,970), [970,2050), [2050,3370). For the fifth OR run `early-fifth.cjs 6 FROM END` on [0,756), [756,1807), [1807,2666), [2666,3370), then run `audit-early-29.cjs`.

## Nine OR gates

With seven hidden ANDs every helper must be true on at least five positive rows, as removing it would otherwise leave nine targets for six helpers. In particular no physical hidden AND can directly read a raw point. After choosing a pair of raw or helper atoms for each terminal, any point absent from the hidden network would force its four incident targets to read that raw point directly, each paired with a distinct low helper. The rank bounds allow at most three such targets in total. Thus all thirteen raw points enter the hidden OR network. Counting its physical ports forces at least 26−2h points to be read just once at an OR of two raw points. Each such point forces a distinct target paired with a low helper: on the line through the two raw inputs, dropping the singly read point preserves the other OR input and hence the entire hidden network. Consequently 26−2h≤3 and h≥12 when there are only seven helpers. See `../plane920-math/seven-raw-ports.md` for the full argument, including terminal rewiring at the same OR budget.

## Remaining cases

**Only h=8 can reach size 29.** At h=7, resolving three ORs cannot retain six targets: six complete targets require at least ten helpers when at most four ORs remain, but only nine are available. The six-target proof in `../plane920-search/four-or-six-nine.md` checks 1,675,184 fourth-OR input pairs and 7,661 fully charged shared libraries without a survivor. It handles rare arms using the five-target lower bound in `../plane920-search/three-or-six-lines-29.md`. By the three-OR classification in `../plane920-search/branch-game.md`, the only remaining first-three prefixes are line trees. The 5,616 checked point permutations give exactly two representatives in the unrestricted list: 3334 (orbit size 78) and 592 (size 156). Their 95 fourth-OR continuations produce 83,676 fifth-OR representatives with two survivors. The 2,237 and 538 sixth-OR representatives of these survivors were excluded with zero cutoffs. `unrestricted/h7-tree-join-audit.json` records the exact parent/child joins.

For h=8, 1,078 three-OR roots yield 1,467,557 fourth-OR representatives, with 3,812 left. The fifth-level h=8 results are still partial; all capped cases remain open. These residual cases prevent claiming a lower bound of 30. The physical raw-port constraints on this case are in `../plane920-math/eight-raw-anchors.md`.

The newer seven-target, five-OR, eight-helper exclusion in `../plane920-search/seven-lines-five-ors.md` implies that resolving the first three ORs cannot retain seven targets. Of the 1,078 roots, 436, 391, 144 and 17 admit a resolution retaining respectively 7, 8, 9 and 10 rows. The remaining 90 roots include the two line trees retaining four and 88 roots retaining six. Their fourth-level completions include only 2,266 of the original 3,812 unresolved paths. `updated-four.cjs` checks this reduction using improved small-family prices; its receipt is `unrestricted/updated-four-29-8.json`. `updated-five.cjs FROM END` reuses prior fifth-level path records and tests the stronger prices without regenerating previously tested extensions.
