# Necessary shape at exactly 28 gates

In the sparse dual, there are thirteen distinct isolated AND terminals. At total size 28 the hidden AND budget is 15−h for h dual OR gates. The unrestricted lower bound 28 and its assumptions are in twenty-eight.md.

The OR count h is at least four by the earlier 29-gate bound for h≤3. With h=4 a nonexceptional first three ORs can be resolved to retain six targets, forcing twelve hidden ANDs with at most one OR. Eleven are available. The exceptional case is included in the 234 trials in four-tail.json. The free-OR lower bound of seven hidden ANDs rules out h≥9.

For h=5,6,7, resolving the first three ORs to keep six lines would leave respectively 2, 3, 4 ORs with only 10, 9, 8 hidden ANDs. All three six-target families are excluded regardless of the six chosen lines, as documented in ../plane920-search/six-lines-28.md. Consequently the first three ORs form a pure four-leaf OR tree on one projective line. The geometric reduction from branch-game.md covers nonpure input cones as well. The reference program enumerates every binary merge order on each of the thirteen lines; this gives 234 programs. Explicit transport by the checked plane group partitions them into two orbits of sizes 78 and 156. Permutations preserve the ordered list of ORs with an unordered pair of inputs at each gate.

tree-extension.cjs enumerates further gates modulo the stabilizer of the complete current prefix. Each normalized operand function is a conjunction of an optional raw point and a subset of preceding ORs. This uses the original minimum-gate, then minimum-OR normalized representative. It removes duplicate truth functions, globally bypassable gates and pairs whose arms are comparable on the thirteen positive rows. The orbit size times the new stabilizer equals the old stabilizer at every new operand pair, and the sum over all pair orbits is explicitly checked against the unquotiented count. Without imposing a forest or cycle assumption, h=5 fails by the fourth OR (50 additional orbits), and h=6 by the fifth (26,354 orbits at that step). For h=7 there remain 1,099 fifth-OR orbits.

effective-cut.cjs applies a stronger necessary lower bound after resolving the later ORs. Let S denote the retained lines. For each subset T of S, keep the anchors private to each line outside T out of its available helper count, and also charge every distinct fixed shared AND operand that is false on all of T. These actual gates can be removed on that positive subfamily without damaging its correctness. If a fixed OR has one arm which covers the other on T, bypass it to that arm. Those simultaneous reductions keep every signal's original value on all selected positives and only decrease values elsewhere. The number of irreducible fixed ORs on T bounds the OR count for the resulting subproblem. Use the one-OR strict bound, certified few-OR lower bounds and the general helper lower bounds in the routine baseAnd. Enumerate common choices of the remaining ORs' two positive signatures; when pruning a single history, resolving the unknown gates on that history leaves only raw conjunctions and earlier fixed ORs. Search cutoffs are retained as possible cases. Of the 1,099 h=7 fifth-OR orbits, 1,098 fail directly. The last admits 6,094 normalized sixth operand pairs; testing them all excludes it as well.

Therefore a 28-gate candidate must have precisely eight dual ORs and seven hidden ANDs. The same tests conditionally exclude both three-OR tree shapes for that count: 18,421 possible fifth-OR orbits reduce to 114. All 808,339 sixth operand pairs in those cases (546,490 stabilizer-orbit representatives) fail. Extending this to arbitrary eight-OR circuits still requires a separate prefix reduction or an unconditional exclusion. The unrestricted bound is 28 and the Lean bound is 25.

From this directory with the project Node, reproduce the prefix and stabilizer calculations with tree-extension.cjs 28 5,6,7,8 5. For the h=7 endgame use tree-cut.cjs 28 7 5 and tree-next-cut.cjs 28 7. For the conditional h=8 endgame use the corresponding value 8. The three OR roots and partial prefix counts are stored under unrestricted/tree-28-5678-5.json and the detailed per-case results in the matching tree-cut and tree-next logs.
